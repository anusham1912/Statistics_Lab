Binomial Distribution Explorer

Formula: $P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$ where $n$ is trials, $p$ is probability of success, and $k$ is successes.

Parameters

Must be a positive integer.

Second Moment ($E[X^2]$) 53.55 $\sigma^2 + \mu^2$
